Privacy Policy for SUBNET KING

Effective date: September 11, 2026

SUBNET KING (the "App") is a VLSM subnet calculator developed and published by Nirmal Kumar Deuja ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information.

Information We Collect

The App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

The App does not require an internet connection and does not use advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, user accounts, location services, camera access, microphone access, contacts, or external servers.

Information Stored on Your Device

The App may store the following calculation history locally on your device:

  • Network addresses entered for calculations
  • Department names and requested host counts
  • Calculated IPv4 and IPv6 subnet results
  • Calculation timestamps

This information is used only to display your history inside the App. It is stored in the App's private local storage and is not sent to us.

Deleting Local Data

You can delete saved history using the App's Clear History function. You can also remove all App data through Android settings or uninstall the App.

Backups and Device Transfers

Android may back up or transfer application data according to your device settings and Google's Android backup services. Any backup or transfer is controlled by Android and your device or Google account settings, not by us. We do not have access to your local history or any backup of it.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the App does not collect or transmit personal information, no registration or parental information is required.

Security

We rely on Android's private application storage for locally saved history. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, so you should avoid entering sensitive or confidential information into calculation fields.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's data practices change. The updated version will be published at the same policy URL with a revised effective date.
